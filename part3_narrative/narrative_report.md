\## 2. Text-Only Chart-Choice Justifications



\### Question 1: Which month had the highest total revenue?

\- \*\*Total Revenue Data\*\*: April = INR 419,417.43; May = INR 444,594.25; June = INR 398,055.24.

\- \*\*Recommended Chart\*\*: Single-Series Vertical Bar Chart (Bivariate: Month vs. Total Revenue).

\- \*\*Justification\*\*: A vertical bar chart displays discrete chronological categories clearly. Because there are only three categories (April, May, June), viewers can identify May as the peak within 10 seconds. The y-axis starts at zero to preserve relative revenue proportions without distortion, and no legend is required since only one metric is plotted.



\### Question 2: What percentage share does Ethnic Wear represent of April's total revenue?

\- \*\*Data\*\*: Ethnic Wear = INR 104,520.77 out of INR 419,417.43 total (24.92%).

\- \*\*Recommended Chart\*\*: Horizontal 100% Stacked Bar (or Donut Chart with direct data labels).

\- \*\*Justification\*\*: This represents a part-to-whole univariate distribution. A 100% component bar with direct percentage labels allows stakeholders to verify Ethnic Wear's \~25% share instantly without visual confusion. 3D projection is avoided to prevent perspective distortion, and direct segment labeling eliminates the need for an external legend.



\### Question 3: How do the four regions compare on total revenue?

\- \*\*Data\*\*: North = INR 337,125.46; West = INR 333,106.33; South = INR 316,736.68; East = INR 275,098.45.

\- \*\*Recommended Chart\*\*: Horizontal Bar Chart sorted descending by revenue.

\- \*\*Justification\*\*: A horizontal bar chart facilitates direct comparison between discrete categorical nominal entities (North, West, South, East). Sorting descending ensures the rank order (North leading, East trailing) is immediately obvious within 5 seconds. Baseline zero alignment ensures truthful visual ratios without unnecessary color legends.
